## Railgun Tower

Place **Railgun Tower** through the Benchmark defense painter, Level Editor, or Fortress Defense build menu. It retains the original `laser_tower_001` ID and `DEFINITIONS/LaserTower001.tres` resource path so existing maps and deployment saves load it automatically.

The default beam lasts **3 seconds**, deals **50 damage per second** to each hostile unit it touches, and sweeps at up to **45°/s** while tracking targets. It pierces enemy units and stops at cover or the terrain. The tower needs **4 seconds** to recover after a beam. Its barrel kicks back at ignition and recovers while firing. The beam follows the barrel's live position and angle.

The Railgun now uses a compact two-tone charcoal chassis, faction-colored armor cheeks and emissive power rails, with one split-rail accelerator assembly. The meshes live in `RENDERING/RailgunTowerMeshes.cs` and reuse the Broadside mask shader and shared `TowerArmorGeometry` helpers. The existing yaw, pitch, recoil, 3.8-unit barrel length and beam/muzzle alignment are preserved. Visual validation pending.

Use **Entity Editor → Defenses** to tune minimum and maximum targeting range, damage per second, turn speed and beam duration. The recovery rate is stored as `ShotsPerSecond`, and its inverse is the delay after the beam ends.

## FAB1 command structures

`FABRICATION/CommandStructure.cs` supplies shared beveled geometry and health/construction state for the Citadel and Fabricator001. The Command Sandbox exposes starting Citadel HP in the scene Inspector. `CommandBuildings.cs` reserves grid footprints at placement, updates the shared obstacle field, and checks the gate and holding/rally slots before release. Citadel and Fabricator armour settings live in `DEFINITIONS/`. Both use the shared damage matrix and percentage armour. Destruction hides the building, stops production and removes its footprint from obstacle routing at the end of the simulation tick. The Command Sandbox includes an idle enemy Fabricator as a test target. Citadels, Fabricators and benchmark towers share a default of 1,000 HP. Scene authors can override Citadel health in the Command Sandbox.

Completed Fabricators show a world-space production bar while making or dispensing a batch. The sandbox samples the bar about three times per second. Orange means output is blocked. The construction percentage label remains separate.

Place the command Citadel with `CITADEL/Citadel.tscn`. The Railgun Tower is defined in `DEFINITIONS/LaserTower001.tres` and placed with the benchmark painter's Defenses tool. Its model is generated by `RENDERING/DefenseRenderer.cs`.

The command Citadel uses its existing health and defeat state to gate a paid Worker queue. Workers clear the Citadel using the same release and route checks as Fabricator troops.

## CARBON2 — Extractor structures

A Worker-built `CarbonExtractor` derives from `CommandStructure` / `StructureHealth` and is registered in the existing command structure list. It participates in **the same H Breacher/structure-targeting, damage, destruction, selection and health-display paths** as other buildings, without requiring special H-class target rules. Its collision is already covered by its underlying Carbon Deposit footprint. Its shared faceted rig mesh is created once; no per-worker nodes or hauling simulation are required.

## Broadside Artillery Tower

**Broadside Artillery Tower** is a second placeable defense, available in the benchmark defense painter, Level Editor defense selection, and **Fortress Defense → Ctrl → Build → Broadside Artillery Tower** for **1,450 Carbon**. It has a larger **3×3-cell footprint**, a chamfered charcoal-metal chassis, faction-colored corner and turret armor, recessed side panels and bright trim, a rotating reinforced launcher carriage, and **two long angular rocket barrels** baked into the existing shared MultiMesh batches. Both tower types share the defense cap, terrain and obstacle clearance, and destruction rules. Both barrels angle up 15°. Each shot starts at the raised muzzle and kicks back only the barrel that fired.

The visual pass lives in `RENDERING/BroadsideTowerMeshes.cs` and `RENDERING/BroadsideArmor.gdshader`. Baked RGB weights distinguish neutral metal, faction paint and bright trim; the existing renderer supplies the faction through MultiMesh custom data at placement. The second visual pass adds heavier corner buttresses, a raised command core, wider vented armor pods, reinforced barrel yokes and emissive faction light channels. The trim uses HDR shader emission (no real light sources); a bloom halo depends on the active environment's glow settings. The base, rotating head and two independent barrel transforms, footprint and gameplay stats are unchanged. Other tower meshes and materials are untouched.

The Broadside Artillery Tower reuses normal nearest-valid-enemy acquisition, range checks and angular aiming. It fires **one real arcing rocket per cooldown**, alternating the left and right launcher after a successful pooled launch. Instead of duplicating the Twin Rocket projectile definition, it references **`GAMEPLAY/PROJECTILES/Types/TwinRocket/TwinRocket.tres`** and compiles a separate projectile variant at **1.8× visual size** and **2× explosion radius**. With the rocket unit's current radius of **2.5**, the tower radius is **5.0 world units**. The existing `ProjectileSystem`, `AreaDamage.Radial`, impact rings, terrain interception, obstacle collision, damage rules and faction filtering do all actual combat work.

Editable values in `DEFINITIONS/RocketTower001.tres` include 1,800 HP, an **18–72 world-unit targeting range**, 36 centre damage per rocket, 0.85 rockets/sec and 125°/sec rotation. Direct line of sight is optional for this tower and disabled by default. Its **1.1× projectile arc multiplier** sets the arc cap to 13.2 units (formerly 18) and reduces the rise at all firing distances compared with the previous 1.5× setting. Rockets clear rocks and walls below their path and hit cover that intersects it. The original resource path and `rocket_tower_001` ID keep existing placements compatible.

## Shield Generator

The static support tower now uses the same charcoal-metal / faction armor / emissive trim material as Railgun and Broadside. Its distinct radial reactor crown, layered foundation, four armored emitter pylons and illuminated core are baked in `RENDERING/ShieldGeneratorMeshes.cs` into one existing tower batch. Its 2×2 footprint, 1,250 HP, default 21-unit influence radius and shield mechanics are unchanged.

`RENDERING/ShieldFieldDomeRenderer.cs` draws a separate, lazy compact MultiMesh of shallow open-bottom transparent domes. XZ size uses the compiled shield radius; height is 55% of radius for tactical readability. `RENDERING/ShieldFieldDome.gdshader` uses 1.2% face opacity and up to 12% at grazing edges, no lights, animation, depth writes or shadows. Domes are cosmetic, appear on placement/previews, disappear on destruction/erase/reset, and follow the owning faction. Benchmark → Shields → Show generator domes toggles battle domes independently of soldier shells, and disabling the shield mechanic hides them in battle. On slopes the dome can intersect terrain; the actual field uses XZ distance, not the surface. Owner visual/GPU validation pending.
