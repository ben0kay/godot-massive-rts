## Railgun Tower

Place **Railgun Tower** through the Benchmark defense painter, Level Editor, or Fortress Defense build menu. It retains the original `laser_tower_001` ID and `DEFINITIONS/LaserTower001.tres` resource path so existing maps and deployment saves load it automatically.

The default beam lasts **3 seconds**, deals **50 damage per second** to each hostile unit it touches, and sweeps at up to **45°/s** while tracking targets. It pierces enemy units and stops at cover or the terrain. The tower needs **4 seconds** to recover after a beam. Its barrel kicks back at ignition and recovers while firing. The beam follows the barrel's live position and angle.

The Railgun uses a tapered octagonal gunmetal pedestal, narrow trunnion cradle, aft capacitor bank and one long directed-energy accelerator barrel. Four faceted emissive induction coils surround the central spine, leading to a recessed circular focusing aperture. Faction armor and restrained lighting distinguish the beam turret from the Broadside's bulky twin artillery launchers. The meshes live in `RENDERING/RailgunTowerMeshes.cs` and reuse the Broadside mask shader and shared `TowerArmorGeometry` helpers. The existing yaw, pitch, recoil, 3.8-unit barrel length and beam/muzzle alignment are preserved. Visual validation pending.

The default targeting range is **6–50.4 world units**. Use **Entity Editor → Defenses** to tune minimum and maximum targeting range, damage per second, turn speed and beam duration. The recovery rate is stored as `ShotsPerSecond`, and its inverse is the delay after the beam ends.

## FAB1 command structures

`FABRICATION/CommandStructure.cs` supplies shared beveled geometry and health/construction state for the Citadel and Fabricator001. The Command Sandbox exposes starting Citadel HP in the scene Inspector. `CommandBuildings.cs` reserves grid footprints at placement, updates the shared obstacle field, and checks the gate and holding/rally slots before release. Citadel and Fabricator armour settings live in `DEFINITIONS/`. Both use the shared damage matrix and percentage armour. Destruction hides the building, stops production and replaces its live footprint with temporary movement-only rubble when the shared wreck budget permits. The Command Sandbox includes an idle enemy Fabricator as a test target. Citadels, Fabricators and benchmark towers share a default of 1,000 HP. Scene authors can override Citadel health in the Command Sandbox.

Completed Fabricators show a world-space production bar while making or dispensing a batch. The sandbox samples the bar about three times per second. Orange means output is blocked. The construction percentage label remains separate.

Place the command Citadel with `CITADEL/Citadel.tscn`. The Railgun Tower is defined in `DEFINITIONS/LaserTower001.tres` and placed with the benchmark painter's Defenses tool. Its model is generated by `RENDERING/DefenseRenderer.cs`.

The command Citadel uses its existing health and defeat state to gate a paid Worker queue. Workers clear the Citadel using the same release and route checks as Fabricator troops.

## CARBON2 — Extractor structures

A Worker-built `CarbonExtractor` derives from `CommandStructure` / `StructureHealth` and is registered in the existing command structure list. It participates in **the same H Breacher/structure-targeting, damage, destruction, selection and health-display paths** as other buildings, without requiring special H-class target rules. Its collision is already covered by its underlying Carbon Deposit footprint. Its shared faceted rig mesh is created once; no per-worker nodes or hauling simulation are required.

## Broadside Artillery Tower

**Broadside Artillery Tower** is a second placeable defense, available in the benchmark defense painter, Level Editor defense selection, and **Fortress Defense → Ctrl → Build → Broadside Artillery Tower** for **1,450 Carbon**. It has a larger **3×3-cell footprint**, a chamfered charcoal-metal chassis, faction-colored corner and turret armor, recessed side panels and bright trim, a rotating reinforced launcher carriage, and **two long angular rocket barrels** baked into the existing shared MultiMesh batches. Both tower types share the defense cap, terrain and obstacle clearance, and destruction rules. Both barrels angle up 15°. Each shot starts at the raised muzzle and kicks back only the barrel that fired.

The visual pass lives in `RENDERING/BroadsideTowerMeshes.cs` and `RENDERING/BroadsideArmor.gdshader`. Baked RGB weights distinguish neutral metal, faction paint and bright trim; the existing renderer supplies the faction through MultiMesh custom data at placement. The second visual pass adds heavier corner buttresses, a raised command core, wider vented armor pods, reinforced barrel yokes and emissive faction light channels. The trim uses HDR shader emission (no real light sources); a bloom halo depends on the active environment's glow settings. The base, rotating head and two independent barrel transforms, footprint and gameplay stats are unchanged. Other tower meshes and materials are untouched.

The Broadside Artillery Tower reuses normal nearest-valid-enemy acquisition, range checks and angular aiming. It fires **one real arcing rocket per cooldown**, alternating the left and right launcher after a successful pooled launch. Instead of duplicating the Twin Rocket projectile definition, it references **`GAMEPLAY/PROJECTILES/Types/TwinRocket/TwinRocket.tres`** and compiles a separate projectile variant at **1.8× visual size** and **2× explosion radius**. With the rocket unit's current radius of **2.5**, the tower radius is **5.0 world units**. The existing `ProjectileSystem`, `AreaDamage.Radial`, impact rings, terrain interception, obstacle collision, damage rules and faction filtering do all actual combat work.

Editable values in `DEFINITIONS/RocketTower001.tres` include 1,800 HP, an **18–72 world-unit targeting range**, 36 centre damage per rocket, 0.85 rockets/sec and 125°/sec rotation. Direct line of sight is optional for this tower and disabled by default. Its **1.1× projectile arc multiplier** sets the arc cap to 13.2 units (formerly 18) and reduces the rise at all firing distances compared with the previous 1.5× setting. Rockets clear rocks and walls below their path and hit cover that intersects it. The original resource path and `rocket_tower_001` ID keep existing placements compatible.

## Shield Generator

The static support tower now uses the same charcoal-metal / faction armor / emissive trim material as Railgun and Broadside. Its distinct radial reactor crown, layered foundation, four armored emitter pylons and illuminated core are baked in `RENDERING/ShieldGeneratorMeshes.cs` into one existing tower batch. Its 2×2 footprint, 1,250 HP, current 28.98-unit influence radius uses the same shared shield mechanics.

`RENDERING/ShieldFieldDomeRenderer.cs` draws a separate, lazy compact MultiMesh of shallow open-bottom transparent domes. XZ size uses the compiled shield radius; height is 55% of radius for tactical readability. `RENDERING/ShieldFieldDome.gdshader` uses 1.2% face opacity and up to 12% at grazing edges, no lights, animation, depth writes or shadows. Domes are cosmetic, appear on placement/previews, disappear on destruction/erase/reset, and follow the owning faction. Benchmark → Shields → Show generator domes toggles battle domes independently of soldier shells, and disabling the shield mechanic hides them in battle. On slopes the dome can intersect terrain; the actual field uses XZ distance, not the surface. Owner visual/GPU validation pending.

## Prefab Shelter — habitation variants

The non-firing, destructible `prefab_shelter` retains its 500 HP, Fortified armor and existing targeting rules. Its default footprint is now 2×2 cells (4×4 world units on the standard grid), down from 3×2; existing placements resolve against the new footprint without a save-format change. Check previously authored shelter layouts after loading, since narrower footprints may change occupied cells/centers depending on the placement format. Three taller living-quarter meshes (sloped-roof habitation, capped barracks and asymmetric service habitat) use full-footprint foundations, dark metal walls, airlocks, windows, vents and faction accent panels. Height is approximately 2.33–2.52 world units at default base height 1. The variant is pseudo-randomly selected by a stable hash of the occupied grid origin; it does not change on editor refresh, reload or unrelated placement edits. Replacing a shelter on the same cell selects the same design. Each variant is lazily cached and batched; destruction, reset and damage cracks use the matching variant batch. Visual height does not change existing projectile/LOS obstacle-height rules. Owner visual and layout validation pending.

## Twin Minigun Tower

`DEFINITIONS/TwinMinigunTower.tres` adds a compact anti-infantry defense with 2×2 cells, 1,000 HP, 3–39 range, 4 damage per round, 12 total rounds/s alternating between two short rotary-style barrels (6 per side), 180°/s turn rate and 650 Carbon cost. These are provisional balance defaults. Uses the existing MinigunRound projectile, no splash or arc, shared acquisition and pooled projectile hit logic. Direct bullets ignore only their firing footprint and stop on other cover or terrain. Three lazy MultiMesh batches reuse the two-tone armor shader; paired barrels pitch together and small flashes reuse the existing pool. No spinning animation or per-tower processing nodes. Registered in the default catalogue, Benchmark/Level Editor previews and Fortress Defense build menu. Owner Godot validation pending.

### Defense firing feedback

Railgun ignition and Twin Minigun Tower muzzle flashes now use 1.9× and 1.0× the shared starburst size (formerly 1.0× and 0.45×). Broadside successful launches also emit a 2.6× starburst on the actual firing muzzle in addition to their existing projectile launch effects. All tower starbursts last 0.16 seconds. Broadside additionally creates a faction-tinted 7-unit ground halo below the launch point that expands slightly and fades over 0.32 seconds; it uses one lazy 64-instance batch and the shared projectile ground-glow shader. Local terrain slope is sampled at launch; sharply uneven terrain may clip the flat glow. No real lights or gameplay changes.

### Railgun beam illumination

The beam and endpoint flares now retain a more pronounced faction tint, including the bright core. A 3.6-unit-wide additive ground streak follows the actual cover/terrain-clipped beam for its full firing duration and disappears on beam end, tower destruction or reset. It uses one lazy shared MultiMesh batch with at most 12 strips per live beam (one on flat/no-heightfield maps), samples terrain at strip boundaries, and fades with beam height. Both ends use a soft rounded capsule falloff over the outer 12% of beam length, including the brighter central streak, without extending past the clipped endpoints. Strongly uneven ground can still clip the approximated strips. No real lights or combat changes.

## Guided anti-air towers

Both towers are aircraft-only defenses with vertical-launch missiles using the existing Interceptor infantry missile pool and bounded AirSpatialGrid queries. **Missile Barrage Tower**: 2×2 cells, 1,100 HP, 34-unit horizontal range (slightly under half Broadside's 72), twelve-round magazine at 6 launches/s, 8-second reload after the final shot, 26 damage per missile, 950 Carbon. **Long-Range AA Tower**: 3×3 cells, 1,800 HP, 95-unit range, one missile every 6 seconds, 85 damage, 2.2× missile visual scale, 1,800 Carbon. These are provisional values. Both use the same existing guided flight speed, turn rate, lifetime, hit test, terrain collision and air damage rules as infantry. No ground damage or separate projectile engine. Full pools or missing targets do not consume magazine rounds; firing pauses and retries at 0.2 seconds. Air kills credit the source tower.

The two static, shared armor meshes display twelve launch cells or one large raised silo and launch from their modeled mouths. Existing catalog, level/benchmark painting, range/kill inspection, destruction, and Entity Editor workflows are reused. Fortress Defense placement and costs are registered, but its current wave runtime does not spawn AirSquadron aircraft: AA combat is active in existing aircraft-enabled Benchmark/Campaign/Skirmish battles, not ground-only waves. No new air-wave system is introduced. Runtime/visual validation pending.

### Reactive building visuals

Barrage socket lights indicate the actual remaining magazine and restore on reload. Railgun induction coils brighten white from rear to front as the real recovery cooldown completes; they remain bright while firing. Existing faction panels and damage cracks are preserved. Shield coverage domes have faint upward faction-colored waves. Damaged buildings at or below 25% health emit dark pooled smoke and occasional electrical glints; damaged tower base lights flicker; destruction emits a scaled visual-only burst, fragments, scorch mark and smoke through the existing effect pools. No extra damage; temporary blocking husks are owned separately by WreckField. Owner visual validation pending.

### Optional shell casing ejection

Broadside Artillery alone enables `EjectShellCasings` by default, with `ShellCasingScale = 1`. A successful alternating shot ejects a brass casing from the matching rotating receiver side. Other ground projectile towers can opt in under Entity Editor → Defenses (Eject shell casings, Casing size). Beams and guided AA launchers do not use this hook. Casings are purely visual, with no ammo or balance change.

### Burnt building husks

Destroyed authored defenses and Fortress Defense structures leave a low, charred, collapsed shared mesh and retain their former footprint for 30 seconds when the shared 32-wreck budget permits. Tier 4 and smaller units route around it; larger units can crush it like other heavy wrecks. Rubble prevents building over it until cleared. The usual explosion, smoke and scorch effects remain. Rubble is movement-only, not a live structure or projectile cover; underlying Carbon deposits remain solid. Owner visual/navigation validation pending.

## Light Barricades

Light Barricades are authored defensive paint with 70 HP per segment. Hostile light ground units move at 25% speed during contact and deal 7 HP per second of traversal. Tier 2 and larger units crush a segment on contact at 75% speed for the contact step. Friendly ground units and aircraft pass freely. Tune health, damage per second, height, speeds and the crush threshold on WorldPaint data.

The stationary Shield Generator reuses the Mobile Generator's animated reactor geometry and shader include. Only the central exposed core animates; the tower's existing armor, pylon trim, dome and collision remain unchanged. No additional draw call or per-tower state.
