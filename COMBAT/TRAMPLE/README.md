# Trample

Diamond cavalry uses this shared melee ability. Enable **Trample Enabled** on another melee definition to reuse it. Each unit definition can tune run-up distance, charge distance, efficiency, damage multiplier, maximum hits, and resistance to incoming charges. Efficiency is starting momentum. Resistance drains momentum and reduces impact damage.

Diamond cavalry takes 3 seconds of unobstructed acceleration to reach its unchanged 9-unit-per-second top speed. The acceleration time is editable on the Diamond definition. The default cavalry needs 2 world units of actual forward run-up and at least 90% of its top speed. Near a living target, it locks its heading and charges for up to 5.5 units. The sweep checks current enemy positions in contact order using both soldiers’ compiled footprint radii. UNIT1 body sizing affects contact and clearance. Run-up and charge travel distances remain ability settings. Each defender can be hit once and the default cap is three distinct hits. After hitting the cap, a charger can finish crossing those contacts within its remaining distance, but a new contact stops it. Allies keep ordinary separation.

Circles cost 0.22 momentum, Squares 0.45 and Pentagons 1.20. The default cavalry starts at 1.0. Cavalry now has 65 HP and 14 base melee damage; its explicit trample damage multiplier is 1.0×, so charge impact is based on 14 damage before resistance and armour rather than its previous 25 × 1.35. The trample formula, starting momentum, victim limit, charge distance and penetration rules remain unchanged. A defender that consumes the remaining momentum stops the charge at contact, takes a reduced impact, and stays in place from the impact itself. Other surviving defenders receive a small shove capped at 0.16 units per tick. Normal melee resumes after the charge tick. A 0.30-second movement recovery and a fresh run-up are required before charging again.

For an owner check, paint cavalry with open ground before several rows of Circles, then repeat with Squares and a Pentagon at the front. Try a point-blank spawn to confirm it opens with regular melee. A row's actual penetration depends on footprint spacing, approach angle and moving defenders.
