# Audio

Weapon sounds, alerts, ambience, and music.


## Future audio ideas
- **Weapon and impact layers:** Distinct light infantry, artillery, railgun, rockets and heavy-vehicle sounds; avoid a sound source per soldier.
- **Terrain ambience:** Forest wind, shallow water, geothermal eruptions, volcanic rumble and optional weather loops. Use a few shared regional emitters or ambience beds.
- **Battle cues:** Restrained faction-neutral alerts for incoming reinforcements, objectives and victory/defeat.
- **Music:** Optional skirmish and campaign tracks with separate music/effects volume controls.

These are optional backlog suggestions; choose priorities and audio assets with the owner. Limit concurrent voices, use distance/priority culling and avoid simulation coupling.
