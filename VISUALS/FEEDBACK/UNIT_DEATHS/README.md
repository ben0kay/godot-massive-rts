# DEATH1 — Soldier death feedback

Lethal damage removes the soldier from gameplay immediately and captures its grounded position, type, faction and facing for presentation. The living army renderer removes dead instances on its normal sync. A separate renderer reuses the shared soldier meshes and faction colours, shrinking each body toward its ground point over 0.20 seconds.

Four slightly larger **faction-colored** geometric puffs move outward, rise and fade over 0.28 seconds. They use the defeated unit's blue, red or green faction color, rather than a universal dark red. Each puff's base scale remains 0.09–0.155, with a brief expansion. The four directions are distributed around the death point with a deterministic per-death rotation. The brief-effect pool caps simultaneous deaths at 512 and puffs at 2,048.

Each death also leaves one **small, irregular, faction-colored ground splat** using a shared horizontal low-poly mesh. Its diameter is kept modest relative to the dying unit's footprint, and it sits 0.028 world units above the final sampled ground position. It lasts **3 seconds**, staying visible initially and fading over its final 1.2 seconds. A separate pool caps simultaneous splats at **1,024**; excess marks are skipped when full, without affecting gameplay or other effects. All puffs use one MultiMesh batch and all splats use another, with a shared unshaded per-instance color shader. No per-death scene nodes, decals or physics bodies are allocated.

Use **Display and controls → Death effects** to disable both puffs and splats and clear their pools. Pause freezes both lifetimes. Clearing or restarting a battle removes all marks. Positions use the lethal-hit ground elevation; splats are flat and may intersect strongly sloping terrain. A full brief-effect pool can still emit splats until the independent splat cap is reached.
